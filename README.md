Yulu Business Case Study – Data Analysis

Problem Statement

Yulu, a micro-mobility service provider, wants to understand the key factors affecting the demand for shared electric cycles.
The goal is to analyze historical data and identify patterns that can help improve business decisions and demand forecasting.

Dataset

The dataset contains information such as:

Datetime
Season, weather conditions
Temperature, humidity, windspeed
Number of users (casual & registered)


Approach

Data Cleaning
Converted datetime into useful features (hour, day, month)
Checked for missing values and inconsistencies
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Analyzed demand trends across:
Hours of the day
Weekdays vs weekends
Seasons and weather conditions
Visualized relationships between features and demand
Feature Engineering
Extracted time-based features
Segmented users into casual and registered categories
Statistical Analysis
Studied impact of variables like temperature, humidity, and weather
Identified correlations with demand


Key Insights

Demand peaks during rush hours (morning & evening) indicating office commute usage
Registered users dominate usage compared to casual users
Demand is higher during pleasant weather and moderate temperatures
Extreme weather conditions (rain, humidity) reduce usage significantly
Weekdays show more consistent demand compared to weekends

Business Recommendations

Increase bike availability during peak commute hours
Optimize pricing or offers for low-demand periods (midday / bad weather)
Focus marketing on registered users for retention
